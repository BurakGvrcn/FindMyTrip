About Us

Our Company

FindMyTrip was founded with a passion for exploration. Our mission is to connect travelers with breathtaking destinations around the globe. We believe travel broadens the mind and enriches the soul, and our platform is designed to make discovering your next getaway easier than ever.

Team Guidance & Instructions

For our Team Members: Always prioritize user experience. When adding new destinations to our database, ensure images are high-quality and descriptions are accurate and engaging. Maintain the integrity of our code by pushing changes to the staging branch before updating the main repository. Customer inquiries should be addressed within 24 hours.
